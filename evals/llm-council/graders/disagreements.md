This grader checks one thing: disagreements. The judge sees only the answer. For reference: the question was "Should a two-person research team store its literature notes in plain markdown files under git, or in a notes app with a database (Notion-style)?" No other models or sub-agents were available, so one model played every seat.

PASS only if the judge section lists at least one disagreement between the seats with an argument given for each side of it.
