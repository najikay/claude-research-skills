**Abstract.** We present SwiftNav, a planner for drone swarms that significantly outperforms all existing methods and runs in real time on embedded hardware.

**Method.** SwiftNav combines a consensus step with a learned collision-avoidance module (CAM). We believe CAM is the key to its performance.

**Experiments.** We simulate 12 drones in a 40 m arena for one run with seed 7. SwiftNav reaches a formation error of 0.42 m. A potential-field baseline with its default parameters reaches 0.47 m. We therefore conclude that SwiftNav is significantly better and will work in real outdoor deployments.

**Conclusion.** SwiftNav is the new state of the art for swarm planning.
